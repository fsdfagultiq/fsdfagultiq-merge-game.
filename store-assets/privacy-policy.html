Privacy Policy

Dim Sum Drop: Night Market

Effective date: 27 November 2026

The short version

  • No account, no login, no chat. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device. We do not receive it.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage data to show and measure ads, based on the choices you make in the consent message.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play (or Apple). We never see your card or payment details.
  • You can change your ad privacy choices any time: Settings → Privacy choices.

2Data the App itself handles

The App works fully offline. It stores the following only on your device (in the App's local storage):

  • Game progress: best score, coins, gems, unlocked dishes (Discovery Album), stall upgrades, selected theme, boosters.
  • Daily missions and login-streak dates.
  • Settings such as sound, music and haptics.
  • A local record of which in-app items you own (for example, whether "Remove Ads" is active).

This information is not sent to us or to any server we operate. We do not run our own analytics, we have no user accounts, and the App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

4Consent and your privacy choices

We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a Google-certified consent management tool. Depending on where you are:

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before personalized ads are shown, you will see a consent message (GDPR / IAB TCF). You can accept, reject or choose specific purposes. If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalized or limited ads, which use data such as approximate location and device information for delivery, frequency capping and fraud prevention.
  • US states with privacy laws (for example California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut and others): you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising.

To change your choice at any time: open the game, tap Settings, then Privacy choices. This reopens the consent form so you can update or withdraw consent.

5In-app purchases

Optional purchases (such as "Remove Ads", "Starter Gems" and "Starter Bundle") are processed entirely by Google Play Billing on Android, or by Apple's App Store on iOS. We do not receive or store your name, card number, billing address or other payment details. The store tells the App only which product was bought so it can be unlocked on your device. Paid items never contain random rewards.

Payment data is governed by Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) or Apple's (apple.com/legal/privacy). Refunds are handled by the store you purchased from.

7Data retention and deletion

  • On-device game data stays until you delete it. Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage (Settings → Apps → Dim Sum Drop → Storage → Clear data), permanently deletes it. We cannot restore it, because we never had a copy.
  • Advertising data is retained by Google according to its policies (policies.google.com/technologies/retention). You can reset your Advertising ID and manage Google ad settings at myadcenter.google.com.
  • If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer it and then delete it. You can ask us to delete it any time.
